Patch Notes

What has changed in the demo, newest first. Thank you to everyone sending feedback: it shapes every update.

Update 0.1.4

November 16, 2026

  • Fixed things sitting on top of each other in the dungeon. A chest could land in the same spot as a shrine, a note or the stairs. Now every chest, shrine and note gets its own space.
  • Monsters no longer start inside walls, on the stairs or stuck in objects.
  • Tidied the floors: bones and rubble no longer stick out from under the stairs or pile on top of each other, and wall torches no longer hang right next to each other.
  • Dropped loot no longer lands inside objects where it's hard to grab.

Update 0.1.3

November 15, 2026

  • Fixed missing buttons on some phones. The Roll button, your skills and the Talk button could fail to appear, so you couldn't talk to Ganesh or anyone else. The game now puts them back in place whenever your screen settles or changes size.

Update 0.1.2

November 15, 2026

  • The shared chest is easier to use. The chest is now on the left and your backpack on the right, so you can see both at once and move items across without scrolling. Your gold and Carbon sit side by side above them.

Update 0.1.1

November 15, 2026

  • The Rift menu is cleaner. The harder difficulties no longer fill the screen with their lock messages. Tap a locked difficulty to see what you need to beat to open it.
  • Footsteps are softer. Your hero's steps were too sharp and clicky, so they are now quieter and gentler on the ears.

Demo 0.1

November 15, 2026

  • The free demo is out: wake by the river, explore Palli, hear the story so far, and fight through the first depth of the Rift.
  • Play it in your browser on a PC or phone. Nothing to download.
