Patch Notes

What has changed in the demo, newest first. Thank you to everyone sending feedback: it shapes every update.

Update 0.1.27

November 19, 2026

  • Resistances are here. Grand and Heroic gear can now roll Poison Resistance or Malformed Resistance, each cutting that kind of damage (up to 75%).
  • Poison comes from Spitters, the Brood Mother's spit and acid pools, and being poisoned. Malformed damage comes from Wraiths, the Children of the Unshaped, High Shaper Vessa and Ilvaroth's magic.
  • Your resistances are shown on the Character page.

Update 0.1.26

November 19, 2026

  • The shared chest is easier to use: drag items between the chest and your backpack, or right click an item to move it across. Dropping onto a filled slot swaps the two items.

Update 0.1.25

November 19, 2026

  • Hovering the mouse over a skill on your skill bar now shows what it does, its rank, damage, cooldown and mana cost.
  • Gear that gives +1 to all skills now shows in your skill ranks: a skill at 10 with that bonus reads 11, in the skill tree and on the skill bar.

Update 0.1.23

November 18, 2026

  • Twelve new heroic powers can now drop on heroic gear. Six work for any hero: Mana Siphon (hits restore mana), Giantsbane (more damage to bosses and elites), Ironskin (take less damage), Swiftness (faster movement), Chain Spark (critical hits arc lightning to nearby enemies) and Corpse Burst (slain enemies can explode).
  • Six are hero powers, two for each class, and only roll on that hero's gear. Knight: Bulwark (a stronger Shield Bash with a longer stun) and Crusader (War Cry heals you). Mage: Pyre (Fireball splits into three) and Permafrost (frozen enemies take more damage). Rogue: Venom Lord (Venom Coat lasts twice as long) and Phantom (Shadow Step strikes twice).

Update 0.1.19

November 18, 2026

  • The figure behind your gear in the inventory is now a proper body outline, a man's for the Knight and the Rogue and a woman's for the Mage, with each slot lined up on it: helm on the head, chest on the body, boots at the feet.

Update 0.1.18

November 18, 2026

  • The bridge in Palli now reaches all the way to the far bank of the river, in every season.
  • The inventory fits phones better. Held upright, your gear shows above a full-size backpack; held sideways, the gear figure shrinks to fit. Empty gear slots show as boxes, and the figure behind your gear fits its space. The shared chest also stacks its two windows on an upright phone.

Update 0.1.17

November 18, 2026

  • Five new heroic powers can appear on Heroic gear:
    • Wellspring: skills have a 20% chance to cost no mana.
    • Executioner: you deal 40% more damage to enemies below 30% life.
    • Warden's Ward: a ward soaks up damage equal to 15% of your life, and comes back after 8 seconds without being hit.
    • Rime: when you're hit, there's a 25% chance to freeze the enemies around you for 1.5 seconds.
    • Bloodrush: every kill gives you 20% more attack and movement speed for 4 seconds.

Update 0.1.16

November 18, 2026

  • Karmara has a voice. She greets you when you walk up to her forge, and your first talk with her is fully voiced, with a new script. Her gift of potions now comes on its own page.

Update 0.1.15

November 17, 2026

  • Skills cost more mana: about two and a half times as much, and a little more as a skill ranks up. Mana potions matter now. Every hero starts with a bit more mana and gains a bit more each level, while Intelligence brings mana back a little more slowly.
  • The Rogue fights with a dagger in each hand, and both blades add their damage. Each dagger hits a bit softer than before, so a good pair hits harder than one dagger used to.
  • Every hero can wear two rings.
  • New heroes start with a full set of plain gear (weapon, helm, chest, gloves and boots) and step out of Ganesh's door already on their feet.
  • When you fall in the depths, you wake up back in Palli and climb to your feet.
  • The same heroic power no longer stacks. If two pieces you wear share one, only one counts and the other is grayed out.
  • The inventory shows the gear you wear on a figure of your hero, with your backpack beside it. The how-to line that sat underneath moved to Help & FAQ.
  • The shared chest opens in its own window on the left, with your inventory in a second window on the right.

Update 0.1.14

November 17, 2026

  • New: hide the HUD for clean screenshots and videos. On a PC, press H to hide the bars, buttons, map and the names on the ground, and press H again to bring them back (you can change the key in Settings → Controls). On a phone, use Settings → HUD; your controls stay, faint, and the HUD button in the corner brings everything back.

Update 0.1.13

November 17, 2026

  • The lamplight shimmering in Palli's puddles and river no longer shines over your hero, or anyone else standing in front of the water. (From a player report. Thank you!)

Update 0.1.12

November 17, 2026

  • New art for the Mage when she faces down-right or down-left: standing, walking, attacking and her Arcane Slide. (From a player report. Thank you!)

Update 0.1.11

November 17, 2026

  • New: camera zoom. On a PC, roll the mouse wheel or press Z to move the camera in closer to your hero (up to twice as close), and roll back out the same way. On a phone, use Settings → Camera zoom. (A player's idea. Thank you!)

Update 0.1.10

November 17, 2026

  • Fixed the breathing animation. When heroes, monsters and bosses stood still, the inside of their bodies moved up and down while their outline stayed frozen. Now the whole upper body rises and falls together, so they breathe naturally. (From a player report. Thank you!)

Update 0.1.9

November 17, 2026

  • Choosing your hero is smoother. Switching between the Knight, the Mage and the Rogue used to make the screen blink and shift a little. Now it switches instantly and everything stays in place.

Update 0.1.8

November 16, 2026

  • Fixed typing in the Report a Bug / Share an Idea box. Some keys were being read as game controls, so the space bar made your hero roll and some letters opened other menus. Everything you type now goes into the box, and the game's buttons can't be pressed while a menu is open. (Found while testing the report page.)

Update 0.1.7

November 16, 2026

  • New: Report a Bug / Share an Idea. Open the pause menu (or reach the end of the demo) to tell us what went wrong or what you'd like to see. Every report is read, and the changes it leads to will show up here.

Update 0.1.6

November 16, 2026

  • The heroes walk properly on diagonals. Walking at an angle used to look more like skipping. Now the Knight, the Mage and the Rogue take real steps in every direction.

Update 0.1.5

November 16, 2026

  • Monsters now turn to face where they're going. Skeletons, archers, bats and ghouls used to be drawn only from the side, even when walking straight at you. Now you see them from the front, the back and every angle in between.

Update 0.1.4

November 16, 2026

  • Fixed things sitting on top of each other in the dungeon. A chest could land in the same spot as a shrine, a note or the stairs. Now every chest, shrine and note gets its own space.
  • Monsters no longer start inside walls, on the stairs or stuck in objects.
  • Tidied the floors: bones and rubble no longer stick out from under the stairs or pile on top of each other, and wall torches no longer hang right next to each other.
  • Dropped loot no longer lands inside objects where it's hard to grab.

Update 0.1.3

November 15, 2026

  • Fixed missing buttons on some phones. The Roll button, your skills and the Talk button could fail to appear, so you couldn't talk to Ganesh or anyone else. The game now puts them back in place whenever your screen settles or changes size.

Update 0.1.2

November 15, 2026

  • The shared chest is easier to use. The chest is now on the left and your backpack on the right, so you can see both at once and move items across without scrolling. Your gold and Carbon sit side by side above them.

Update 0.1.1

November 15, 2026

  • The Rift menu is cleaner. The harder difficulties no longer fill the screen with their lock messages. Tap a locked difficulty to see what you need to beat to open it.
  • Footsteps are softer. Your hero's steps were too sharp and clicky, so they are now quieter and gentler on the ears.

Demo 0.1

November 15, 2026

  • The free demo is out: wake by the river, explore Palli, hear the story so far, and fight through the first depth of the Rift.
  • Play it in your browser on a PC or phone. Nothing to download.
